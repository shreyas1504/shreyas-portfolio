I wrote and prioritized user stories on a 20-person Agile team, owned the backlog across 10+ launches, cut edge-case defects 25% and escalations 40%, and sat in rooms watching Tenant Admins struggle to configure a product we'd just built. No PM title, but the work was product through and through. How I Think About Products

Three product philosophy pillars shaped from wiring multi-tenant APIs, running enterprise pilots, and shipping consumer apps.

01

Engineers see what PMs miss

Months wiring multi-tenant APIs taught me more about Tenant Admin needs than any persona workshop. The sharpest product insight I've had came from actually building the thing, not from a framework.

02

The admin is your real customer

In B2B, the person signing the check is never the daily user. The workspace admin decides renewal. I watched this play out firsthand watching admins struggle with SMARTeIS configuration across Southeast Asia.

03

Metrics need a narrative

DAU went up 15%. For which users? Which cohort? Numbers only matter when they're connected to a story about behaviour change. I learned that from watching FROSH crash and engagement data after we launched.

I scoped the feature from dealership customer research, authored the PRD and KPIs, gated the release on a controlled 36-account pilot against a concurrent holdout group, benchmarked competing AI models on production data (cutting misdetection from 46.2% to 30.8%), and built the $450K+ ARR business case.
Both at TruVideo and when building Arcus, I designed human-in-the-loop AI evaluation harnesses to benchmark model outputs against raw production ground truth before they reach users making financial or operational decisions - holding 90%+ accuracy in production.
